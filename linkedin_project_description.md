# LinkedIn Project Description

**138/34.5-kV Substation Load Flow, N-1 Contingency, and Capacity Analysis**

Built a conceptual two-transformer 138/34.5-kV substation model in PowerWorld to evaluate normal loading, single-transformer contingencies, corrective bus-tie transfer, and future load growth. The base case was 40 MW at approximately 0.95 PF with each 50-MVA transformer at 42.8% loading. Under either transformer outage, closing the 34.5-kV bus tie increased the surviving transformer to 87.24% while maintaining service. Load-growth testing identified an approximate N-1 thermal boundary near 45.6 MW: +13.9% growth produced 99.94% loading and 0.9597 pu post-contingency voltage, while +14% exceeded the 50-MVA transformer limit.

**Tools/skills:** PowerWorld Simulator, single-line diagram interpretation, load flow, N-1 contingency analysis, transformer loading, voltage checks, scenario-based capacity planning, engineering documentation.

**Scope:** Conceptual portfolio study; excludes protection coordination, short-circuit/arc-flash, grounding, relay settings, detailed physical design, and verified automatic transfer logic.
